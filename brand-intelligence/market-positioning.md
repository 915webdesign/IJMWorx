## 1. Our Position

**IJM Worx is El Paso's full-service outdoor living builder — one team that designs *and* builds your entire backyard, shown to you in 3D before a shovel hits the dirt.** Pools, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, hardscape, lighting, water features, concrete, and landscape design — under one roof, one accountable crew, one number to call. Where most local competitors do a single trade (just pools, or just concrete) and leave you to coordinate the rest, IJM is the craftsman-contractor for the *whole* transformation: licensed since 2007, 4.9 stars across 36 reviews, permits handled, built for the desert. The core claim: **stop juggling contractors and guessing — see your finished backyard first, then have one trusted team build all of it.**

## 2. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor type | What they claim | Where they win | Where they're weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Single-trade pool builders** | "El Paso's custom pool experts" | Pool specialization, brand recognition, focused portfolios | You still need separate pergola, kitchen, concrete & landscape contractors; no unified design; backyard ends up a patchwork |
| **Concrete / landscaping generalists & handymen** | "Affordable concrete & yard work" | Low upfront price, fast to start | Inconsistent quality, no design vision, no high-end features, often unlicensed/uninsured; the source of the "ghosting" horror stories |
| **National lead-gen platforms** (Angi, Thumbtack, Houzz, Networx) | "Get matched with top local pros" | Reach, reviews aggregation, instant quotes | They're not a builder; you get spammed by 5 contractors, race-to-the-bottom bidding, zero accountability |
| **DIY / big-box** (Home Depot install, kit pergolas, Intex) | "Do it yourself and save" | Cheapest, immediate | No custom design, no permits, no craftsmanship, no warranty — you manage everything and live with the result |

## 3. What We Own vs. What's Contested

**Territory we own:**
- **Full-service, one-team outdoor living.** Design + build, every trade under one roof. IJM is the single accountable partner for the entire backyard — the only credible "stop juggling contractors" answer in the market.
- **3D design-first.** "See your yard before we build it." Reviewers cite the 3D walkthrough as the moment they felt confident. Few local competitors offer it; almost none lead with it.
- **Craftsmanship + premium materials.** Tile, plaster, stone, top-of-the-line equipment, with a 4.9-star track record and "feels like family" service. Quality you can see in the finish.
- **Reliability in a category infamous for no-shows.** Reviews repeatedly cite on-time, clean, communicative crews — and clients rescued from contractors who ghosted them. In home services, *trust is the differentiator*, and IJM has the receipts.
- **Local, desert-smart, since 2007.** Handles El Paso city permits and inspections; designs for heat, shade, dust, and water. Not a transplanted national brand.

**Contested territory:**
- **"Custom pools El Paso."** Crowded — several pool builders bid here. This is where IJM currently competes and converts, but winning means leaning on full-service + 3D design + reviews, not just the word "pools."
- **"Free estimates" and "financing."** Table stakes now; many competitors offer both. Necessary, not differentiating on their own.
- **Premium / high-end backyards.** A few competitors also chase the luxury build. IJM wins it on the design-to-build experience and proof, not the claim.

**Territory we've ceded (and that's fine):**
- **Cheapest price / budget DIY.** Not competing on lowest cost. Competing on value, design, craftsmanship, and reliability.
- **Lawn maintenance** (mowing, sprinkler repair, weekly service). IJM builds transformations; it isn't a maintenance company.
- **Commercial / municipal megaprojects.** Focus is residential backyards and outdoor living.

## 4. White Space

**"One contractor for the whole backyard."**
Every competitor is single-trade. No one in El Paso aggressively owns the anti-coordination message. IJM can: *"Stop hiring four contractors for one backyard."*

**"See it in 3D first."**
The big-ticket fear is spending tens of thousands on something you can't picture. 3D design de-risks the decision, and it's underclaimed locally. Own "see your finished backyard before we break ground."

**"Rescued from the bad contractor."**
A real, recurring story in the reviews: homeowners burned by no-shows and unfinished jobs who came to IJM and finally got it done right. Speak directly to the homeowner who's been let down.

**Heat-smart outdoor living.**
Shade + water + misting designed specifically for El Paso summers. A local-relevance angle national and single-trade players don't credibly own.

## 5. Market Shifts

**2026 — Reviews decide who gets the call.** Homeowners vet contractors through Google reviews and the Map Pack more than ever. IJM's 4.9 stars across 36 reviews is a compounding moat — and the reason the GBP and review-response work matters as much as the ads.

**2026 — Financing expands the market.** Monthly-payment framing turns "someday" backyards into "this season" projects. The buyers exist; the budget objection is now a financing conversation, not a dead end.

**Ongoing — Backyard-as-destination.** The post-2020 outdoor-living boom is still strong across the Sun Belt. Pools, pergolas, and outdoor kitchens read as lifestyle investments, not extravagances.

**Media gap — Pool-only ad coverage leaves the field open.** Right now, paid search for IJM covers pool keywords only, so pergolas, outdoor kitchens, hardscape, lighting, water features, concrete, and landscape design are uncontested in local search. That's both a positioning opening and a media opening competitors haven't closed.
