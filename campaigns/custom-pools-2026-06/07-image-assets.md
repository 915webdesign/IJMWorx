# Custom Pools — Ad Image Assets

How to source and shoot the image assets for the Custom Pools Google Ads
campaign. Closes the audit's "no image assets" gap (~+4% CTR opportunity).

## Google Ads specs (the rules)
- **Two crops of each image:** Square **1:1 — 1200×1200** (min 300×300) and
  Landscape **1.91:1 — 1200×628** (min 600×314). Add a Portrait **4:5 — 960×1200**
  too if you want max mobile coverage.
- Up to **20 images**; ≤**5 MB** each; JPG/PNG.
- Real, bright, **subject fills the frame**. **No text, phone numbers, or big
  logos baked in.** No collages, borders, or heavy filters (they get disapproved).
- Google Ads takes **file uploads**, so use your **high-res Drive originals** for
  best quality.

## B. Shot brief — fill these gaps (ranked by payoff)

**1. 3D renders → export as images (highest leverage).**
Pull 3–5 clean renders from your design software (pool + decking + water feature,
no UI chrome). Nobody else shows this, and it nails the "Free 3D Render With Every
Proposal" hook. Crop 1:1 and 1.91:1.

**2. Before / after (dirt → finished).**
Photograph **every** job at the bare-dirt stage from a fixed spot, then the same
angle finished. Use the strong "after" as the ad asset; save true before/after
pairs for the landing page, GBP, and social (Google can flag obvious
before/after collages in Search assets).

**3. Lifestyle / people (emotional lift).**
Family in the pool, friends by the fire feature at dusk, someone on the tanning
ledge. Your reviews are all "family fun / hosting / memories," but every current
photo is an empty pool. 2–3 in-use shots (with client permission) move clicks.

**4. Drone / aerial.**
One overhead of a full backyard transformation — shows scope a ground shot can't.

**5. Golden-hour hero.**
A flagship pool at sunset/twilight — premium, and it matches your gold-on-dark
brand. Great as the lead landscape asset.

### Capture specs (hand to whoever shoots)
- Shoot **landscape and square framing** of each subject (or leave headroom to crop both).
- 1200 px minimum on the short side; bright, midday-clean or golden-hour.
- Subject fills the frame; no text/logo/phone in-frame.
- These same shots feed the **landing page, GBP, and any future Performance Max /
  social** — one shoot, used everywhere.
