# Landing Page Brief — Custom Pools

Send every ad in this campaign to a **dedicated custom-pools page**, not the
generic homepage. Matching the ad to the page lifts Quality Score, lowers CPC,
and converts more of the clicks you're paying for. Recommended slug:
`/custom-pools-el-paso`. Until it's live, the CSVs use `https://www.ijmworx.com/`.

Voice per `brand-intelligence/creator-style.md` — warm, local, transformational,
reassuring; never gatekeeping.

## Page structure

1. **H1 — the outcome:** "Your Custom Pool, Designed in 3D and Built to Last."
2. **Subhead:** El Paso's trusted custom pool builder since 2007 — one team designs, permits, and builds it.
3. **Above-the-fold CTA:** "Get Your Free 3D Pool Design" + tap-to-call 915-922-9618.
4. **The 3D hook (lead with it):** "See your pool in your own backyard before we break ground." IJM's strongest differentiator.
5. **Pool gallery** — real El Paso inground pools, with waterfalls, spas, lighting, decking. No stock.
6. **Reviews** — pull pool-specific 5-star quotes ("transformed my backyard into a relaxing getaway," "from ugly dirt to a desert oasis").
7. **Trust row:** Licensed & insured · Since 2007 · BBB A+ · We handle permits · 4.9 stars, 36 reviews.
8. **Financing:** "Pools from $149/mo," with the application link.
9. **Process in 3 steps:** Free 3D design → We permit & build → Your first swim.
10. **What's included:** design, premium tile & plaster, equipment, decking, permits & inspections.
11. **Service area:** El Paso, Upper Valley, Westside, Northeast, Horizon City, Socorro, San Elizario, Canutillo, Vinton, Sunland Park NM.
12. **FAQ:** timeline, permits, financing, gunite vs. other, "do you do pool + spa / waterfalls?"
13. **Sticky mobile CTA bar** (call + free design) — most traffic is mobile.
14. **Working form** that routes to `ijmworx@gmail.com` **and** fires a conversion (see tracking checklist).

## Conversion priorities
Primary: free-design/estimate form submit + phone call. Make both impossible to
miss on mobile. Speed-to-lead matters — the journey file notes the fast, human
responder wins the job.
