# IJM Worx — Existing Ad: Findings & Action Plan

Based on the **2026-06-13 live audit** of the active Search campaign **"IJM 2"**
(account 592-524-0050). Plain-English plan for Izaak + sequencing for whoever
edits the account.

## The short version
The **ad is good** — leave the creative alone. The problem is **settings**, and
the biggest two leaks come from the area/income targeting. Fix ~5 things and the
same budget should produce more calls at a lower cost per lead.

## What's working — don't touch
- **Ad Strength: Excellent**, no disapprovals, **11.4% CTR** (benchmark ~4%).
- **305 negative keywords** — well maintained. 6 sitelinks + structured snippet, all approved.
- Form + "Call from Ads" conversions are **tracking**.
- **4 leads / 30 days at ~$140 CPA**, 53.6% impression share — a solid base to grow.

## Fix first (highest impact)
1. **Bidding → Maximize Conversions.** It's currently **Maximize Clicks**, so Google is chasing *clicks, not leads*. Switching tells it to go after calls/forms. → lower cost per lead.
2. **Re-include "Unknown" household income.** Income is **Unknown** for most searchers, and it's currently **Excluded** — so the campaign is silently blocking a big slice of qualified local traffic (109 clicks' worth historically). Also reconsider excluding 41–50% / lower-50% in a mid-income city. *This is the single most damaging setting.*
3. **Add a Call asset.** Phone leads are a primary goal but there's **no call button** on the ad. *Izaak action first:* accept the "Call & Messaging Ads Terms" banner, then add the number.
4. **Raise the daily budget.** ~**22.5% of impressions are lost to the $50/day cap** on an already-profitable campaign — you're leaving ~1 in 5 searches on the table. *Izaak action: approve a higher daily budget.*
5. **Add assets:** 4–6 **Callouts** (Licensed & Insured, Since 2007, Free Estimates, Financing Available, Custom Designs, Local El Paso), plus **Image** assets (we already cropped a set) and a **Location** asset (link the Google Business Profile).

## Point the ad at the landing page
6. **Change the ad's Final URL from the homepage (`ijmworx.com`) to the dedicated page (`ijmworx.com/ep-pools`).** The audit shows it's pointing at the homepage today. The dedicated page (single name + phone form) lifts Quality Score and conversion rate.

## Next (medium)
7. **Add top converting search terms as exact keywords** ("pool builders el paso," etc. — converting as close variants but not added).
8. **Conversion hygiene:** fix or remove "Website Call 2" (no recent conversions), and turn on **Enhanced Conversions for Leads** to feed smarter bidding.
9. **Ad rotation → Optimize** (currently "rotate indefinitely"); add **4–6 more headlines** (only 9 of 15 used); review the low-QS "pool companies" keyword.

## The targeting insight (the important one)
Targeting better areas and higher-income buyers is a smart instinct for a
high-ticket pool builder. But the way it's set up is **over-restricting**:
- Excluding **"Unknown" income** blocks most searchers (Google rarely knows income).
- Only **3 ZIPs** (79936/79938/79928) may be tighter than the real service area.
- Combined with the **$50/day cap**, the campaign is throttled on three sides.

Net: loosen the income/area cage and let **Maximize Conversions** find the good
leads, rather than hard-excluding traffic up front.

## Who does what
- **Izaak (owner actions):** accept Call/Messaging Ads Terms · approve a budget increase · confirm the true service-area ZIPs.
- **Account edits (915 or current manager):** bidding strategy · re-include income · assets (callouts/images/location) · Final URL → /ep-pools · keywords · conversion fixes · ad rotation.

## Expected impact (directional, not a guarantee)
Switching to lead-focused bidding and unblocking the income/budget/geo throttles
should **increase lead volume and lower CPA** for the same or a modestly higher
spend. Reassess after ~30 days / 15–30 conversions, then consider tCPA.
