# Custom Pools Campaign — Plan (June 2026)

A new, **dedicated Google Ads Search campaign for custom pools only** — separate
from any other IJM service or existing campaign. Built by the `ppc-ads` skill
from the brand-intelligence files.

## Why pools-only, and why now

- **It's the proven converter.** The April 2026 audit showed IJM's pool keywords
  pulling an **8.92% CTR** (vs. the ~4% home-services benchmark) and producing
  the account's only conversions. Lead with what already works.
- **Highest ticket, clearest intent.** A "pool builder el paso" search is a
  high-value, high-intent buyer. A focused campaign concentrates budget and
  signal where the return is best.
- **Clean and measurable.** One service = one tight campaign that's easy to
  read, optimize, and report on — and easy to expand into other services later
  using the same system.

## Campaign structure

One Search campaign, **5 tightly-themed ad groups** so each ad matches the search:

| Ad group | Intent it captures |
|---|---|
| **Pool Builders** | Core, proven: "pool builders el paso," "el paso pool builders" |
| **Pool Companies / Contractors** | "pool companies el paso," "pool contractor el paso" |
| **Custom & Inground Pools** | "custom pools," "inground pool builder," "gunite pool" |
| **Pool Design / 3D** | "pool design el paso," "3d pool design" — leans on IJM's differentiator |
| **Pool Financing** | "pool financing," "affordable pools," "monthly payment pool" |

Full keywords + ads in `02-ad-groups-keywords-and-ads.md`.

## Targeting

- **Locations:** El Paso (Upper Valley, Westside, Northeast), Horizon City, Socorro, San Elizario, Canutillo, Vinton (TX), Sunland Park (NM). Set to **Presence** ("people in your targeted locations").
- **Networks:** Search only — turn **off** Search Partners and Display Expansion to start.
- **Language:** English + Spanish.
- **Ad schedule:** business hours + early evening to start, unless after-hours calls are answered.

## Budget & bidding

- **Bidding:** start **Maximize Clicks** for the learning phase, then move to
  **Maximize Conversions / Target CPA (~$120–$150)** once conversion tracking is
  fixed and ~15–30 conversions have accrued.
- **Budget:** recommend **$40–$70/day** concentrated on pools to start (the
  account has run $60–$130/day across broader targeting). Final number is
  Izaak's call — scale up once CPA is proven.
- **Match types:** exact + phrase on the proven, high-intent terms; add broad
  only later alongside conversion-based bidding and the negative list.

## Keeping the campaign pure

Apply the existing master negative list **plus cross-service negatives** (pergola,
outdoor kitchen, hardscape, concrete, landscaping, lighting, hot tub, etc.) so
this campaign only spends on pool intent and never competes with a future
service campaign. See `03-negative-keywords.md`.

## Creative strategy (the voice)

Warm, local, transformational — never gatekeeping. Every ad leans on the three
things that close pool buyers (per the journey file):

1. **Free 3D design** — "see your pool before we build it" (de-risks the spend).
2. **Reliability** — licensed since 2007, 4.9★/36 reviews, "crews that show up" (kills the ghosting fear).
3. **Financing from $149/mo** — turns "someday" into "this season."

## Assets

Call (accept Call/Messaging terms first), location (link GBP), pool-focused
sitelinks (Pool Gallery, Financing, Free 3D Design, About, Contact), callouts,
structured snippets, a financing **price asset**, and **6–10 real pool photos**
(Google flagged images as a +4% CTR opportunity). Details in
`04-extensions-and-assets.md`.

## Landing page

Point ads at a **dedicated custom-pools page**, not the generic site — this lifts
Quality Score and conversion rate. Brief in `05-landing-page-brief.md`. Until
it's live, the CSVs use `https://www.ijmworx.com/` as a placeholder.

## Before launch

Fix conversion tracking first (the audit found "WEBSITE CLICK CALLS" broken and
only ~5 of 23+ leads tracked). Full pre-launch list in
`06-tracking-and-launch-checklist.md`.

## Expected impact (directional, not a guarantee)

Concentrating budget on the proven pool terms, with fixed tracking, a dedicated
landing page, and full assets, should improve **conversion rate and CPA** versus
the current setup — and make every dollar's result visible. Reassess after
~30 days and 15–30 conversions, then decide on scaling budget or spinning up the
next service campaign (pergolas or outdoor kitchens are the natural next steps).
