# Landing Page Briefs — one page per service

The audit flagged two linked problems: **no service-specific landing pages**
(every ad points to one generic page) and a **low Quality Score** on "pool
companies" from ad-to-page mismatch. Sending each ad group to a matching page
lifts Quality Score, lowers CPC, and converts better.

Until these exist, the CSVs point ads at `https://www.ijmworx.com/`. Build the
pages below, then swap each ad group's Final URL.

---

## Shared template (every service page)

Apply the voice in `brand-intelligence/creator-style.md` — warm, local,
transformational, reassuring; never gatekeeping.

1. **H1 — the outcome, not the service.** e.g. "Your Backyard, Finally a Place You Never Want to Leave."
2. **Subhead** naming the service + city + the one-team promise.
3. **Primary CTA above the fold:** "Get Your Free 3D Design" (+ tap-to-call 915-922-9618).
4. **The 3D design hook** — "See your yard before we build it." This is IJM's strongest differentiator; lead with it.
5. **Real project gallery** for *that* service (no stock).
6. **Reviews specific to that service** — pull from the 4.9★ / 36-review set.
7. **Trust row:** Licensed & insured · Since 2007 · BBB A+ · We handle permits · 4.9 stars.
8. **Financing:** "From $149/mo," with the application link.
9. **"One team, whole backyard"** cross-sell strip linking the other services.
10. **Process in 3 steps:** Free 3D design → We handle permits & build → Enjoy your oasis.
11. **Service-area line:** El Paso, Upper Valley, Westside, Northeast, Horizon City, Socorro, San Elizario, Canutillo, Vinton, Sunland Park NM.
12. **Short FAQ:** timeline, permits, financing, "do you do the whole backyard?"
13. **Sticky mobile CTA bar** (call + estimate) — most traffic is mobile.
14. **Working form** that routes to `ijmworx@gmail.com` and fires a conversion (see tracking checklist).
